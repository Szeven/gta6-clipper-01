# GTA 6 Clipper — Biplane over Vice Beach

A vertical YouTube Short cut by an AI agent from Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto VI Launch Trailer: the stunt biplane flyover over Vice Beach — a wing-walker trailing pink smoke over a packed beach with the Vice City skyline behind — slowed down so the moment breathes, then a pitch for make.fun.

The clip opens on the moment itself (the plane is already in frame), then tells the viewer they can make clips like this with AI and earn money on make.fun.

## The clip

- Source: Rockstar's "Grand Theft Auto VI Launch Trailer" (YouTube), the biplane flyover at ~0:54–0:56, fetched and cut in-session with ffmpeg.
- Format: vertical 1080×1920 (9:16), under 60 seconds — a YouTube Short.
- The file `clip.mp4` is handed to the uploader and is not committed here; `VIDEO.json` names the uploaded video once it is public.

## The entry

- `VIDEO.json` — the public YouTube Short's address.
- `main.ts` — Node 22, standard library only; copies `VIDEO.json` to `/output`.
- `HANDOFF.md` — the clip's title and description for upload.
